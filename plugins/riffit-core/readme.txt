=== Riffit Core ===
Requires at least: 6.5
Requires PHP: 8.2
Stable tag: 0.9.0
License: GPLv2 or later
License URI: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-2.0.html
Requires Plugins: bbpress, buddypress

Headless community APIs for feeds, communities, discussions, votes, polls, media, profiles, messages, notifications, and moderation. Configure the frontend URL in Settings > Riffit. Email magic links are supported. WordPress.com Connect requires a dedicated application and backend-only credentials; keep it disabled until configured. Google is deferred.
Activation enables the BuddyPress groups, messages, profiles, activity, and notifications components.
Uninstall preserves content and voting data.
